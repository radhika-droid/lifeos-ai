# 🧠 LifeOS AI

> Your intelligent personal assistant for **better decisions, productivity, and life management**.

---

## 🚀 Overview

**LifeOS AI** is an AI-powered system designed to help users **make smarter decisions and manage their daily life efficiently**.

Instead of just tracking tasks, LifeOS AI analyzes your habits, priorities, and goals to provide **intelligent suggestions and decision support** — acting like a **personal AI advisor**.

---

## 🧠 Core Idea

> “Don’t just organize your life — optimize your decisions.”

LifeOS AI helps answer questions like:

* ❓ *Should I study now or rest?*
* ❓ *How should I plan my day for maximum productivity?*
* ❓ *What habits are affecting my progress?*
* ❓ *What should I prioritize today?*

---

## ✨ Features

* 📅 Smart Task & Schedule Management
* 🧠 AI-Based Decision Support System
* 📊 Productivity & Habit Analysis
* 🎯 Priority Recommendation Engine
